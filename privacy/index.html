Privacy Policy — Encore

Developer: Katla
Effective date: October 9, 2026
Contact: foku.assistant@gmail.com

Encore is a festival companion app that shows festival lineups and schedules, and lets you save favorites and set reminders. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. Encore does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

  • Favorites, reminders, and settings are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • Festival content (lineups, schedules) is downloaded from public sources so the app can display it. These downloads do not include or transmit any information about you.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — only to deliver reminders you explicitly set for festival sets.
  • Exact alarms — to fire those reminders at the right time.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after your device restarts.
  • Internet access — to download festival lineup and schedule content.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties, because we do not collect any data.

Children

Encore is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
